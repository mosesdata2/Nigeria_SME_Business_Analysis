# Nigerian SME Business Analysis

## Project Overview

A Power BI business analysis project examining 10,000 Nigerian SMEs to understand financial performance, customer activity, business presence, and city-level opportunities.

## Business Questions

- Which industries show the strongest financial performance?
- Which industries have the highest customer activity?
- Which cities have the largest SME business activity?
- How does profitability vary across industries and cities?
- What business opportunities can be identified from the data?

## Tools Used

- PostgreSQL / SQL
- Power BI
- DAX

## Dashboard Pages

1. Financial Performance
2. Customer Demand & Business Activity
3. City Performance & Opportunity
4. Executive Insights

## Key Findings

- Manufacturing showed the strongest overall financial performance.
- Hospitality recorded the highest customer activity per SME.
- Education had the largest SME and active-business presence.
- Lagos recorded the highest total revenue and active SME count.
- Profitability varied across industry-city combinations.

## Project Workflow

Dataset → SQL Analysis → Power BI & DAX → Dashboard → Business Insights
